# Rapid Fire 2.0: Crimson Arena, Phoenix Diamond, Gang aur Powerful Entry

Rapid Fire ko ek powerful, khoobsurat game arena banana hai: gehra crimson-laal background, ubhre hue teal buttons, 3D chamakdaar icons aur arena ki apni neeche wali bar (Home, Trophy, Arena, Gang, Store).
Lagatar sahi jawab par Phoenix Diamond jal uthta hai aur diamonds jeete jaate hain. Har player ki profile, friends aur gang honge; ek tap mein ek dost, kai dost ya poore gang ko challenge (ek match mein 5 players tak), aur har match powerful entry show se shuru hoga: har player ko 3.5 second.

## Kiske liye
- JEE ke students jo chhote, tez quiz duels khelkar formulas aur concepts pakke karte hain.
- Wo students jo apne doston (class, coaching, hostel) ke saath competition chahte hain, apna gang banana chahte hain, aur naye players ko dost banana chahte hain.

## Core features aur experience

### 1. Crimson arena look
- **Background:** gehra crimson / maroon laal. Us par usi rang mein halke, bade nishaan (diamond, atom, bijli, formula ke chinh) aur kinaaron par halka andhera. Sawaal wali screen par ye nishaan aur dheeme, taaki sawaal saaf rahe.
- **Buttons:** dabane wale saare buttons teal. Mote aur ubhre hue: upar halki chamak, neeche gehra kinaara, dabane par neeche dabte hain. Text safed, mota, gehre outline ke saath.
- **Text:** headings sunehre-peele aur mote, chhoti line halki aqua, baaki safed.
- **Sona:** sirf rewards, jeet, khaas frame aur ribbon.
- **Badge:** ginti wale badge chamakte peele, taaki laal par saaf dikhein. Online dot hara.
- **Phoenix Diamond:** laal heera, dono taraf aag ke pankh. Ye arena ka nishaan hai aur yahan dikhega:
  - lobby ke beech, bada
  - arena bar ke beech wale button par
  - match mein streak ke dauran (section 5)
  - entry show mein VS ke peeche, aur Victory par
- Saath mein:
  - gehre wine rang ke ubhre hue panel; khaas cheezon par sunehra frame aur ribbon
  - bade bold numbers
  - har screen par ek hi saaf main action
  - main button par halki roshni ki lakeer chalti hai
  - zaroori pal par vibration aur sound
- Ye theme in sab screens par lagega:
  - lobby aur play screen
  - quick match ki talaash, Match Room, entry show
  - result, history, leaderboard
  - store, quests, trophies
  - profile, friends aur gang screens
- Baaki app (Home, tests) light hi rahega.

### 2. 3D chamakdaar game icons
- Patli line wale saare icons hatenge. Unki jagah ubhre hue, rangeen, chamakdaar 3D icons aayenge.
- Poora set:
  - **Bar:** Home, Trophy, Phoenix Diamond, Gang, Store
  - **Modes:** Quick Match (do talwarein), Play Friends, Practice Rival, Solo Sprint (bijli)
  - **Kamaai:** coin, daily streak ki aag, paanch diamonds (Red, Blue, Emerald, Gold, Kohinoor)
  - **Gang ke nishaan:** 8 alag emblem (jaise sher, baaz, bijli, taj, dhaal), jinme se gang apna chunta hai
  - **Baaki:** quests ka chest, weak cards (nishaana), history, Back
- Jahan kuch naya ho, us icon par ginti wala badge.

### 3. Navigation: arena ki apni bar
- Rapid Fire mein aate hi app ki bar ki jagah arena ki bar aati hai. Dono bars mein sirf Home icon ek jaisa hai. Baayein se daayein:

| Jagah | Kya khulta hai |
|---|---|
| **Home** | App ke Home par seedha wapas, ek tap mein. Wahan app ki apni bar laut aati hai. |
| **Trophy** | Teen hisse: **Ranks** (leaderboard), **Rewards** (daily quests, daily streak, Diamond Vault, trophies) aur **My matches** (history). Reward tayyar ho to badge. |
| **Arena** (beech mein, ubhra hua Phoenix Diamond) | Lobby |
| **Gang** | Aapke gangs aur friends ek jagah: gang ya dost ko challenge, naya gang, search, dost bulane ka link, kisne joda, aaye hue challenges. Icon par ginti. |
| **Store** | Avatars, entry effects, frames, arenas aur power-ups, coins se. |

- **Upar ki patti (Arena, Trophy, Gang aur Store par):**
  - avatar, naam aur level ring: tap karne par profile
  - diamonds ki ginti: tap karne par Diamond Vault
  - coins, saath mein +: tap karne par Store
- **Andar ki screens** (profile, gang ki screen, Diamond Vault, chapter picker): upar baayein ubhra hua Back button.
- **Khel ke dauran** (talaash, Match Room, entry show, match, result): bar chhupi rehti hai. Bahar nikalne ke liye Cancel, Leave ya Done.
- **Phone ka Back button:** Trophy, Gang ya Store se lobby; lobby se Home; andar ki screen se ek kadam peeche.
- **App ki apni bar** (Home, Rank, Rapid Fire, My Tests, Mission) jaisi hai waisi rahegi; usme Gang icon nahi judega.

### 4. Nayi lobby (Arena tab)
- Ye ek game lobby jaisa stage hoga, list jaisa page nahi. Upar se neeche:
- **Upar ki patti:** avatar, naam, level ring, diamonds aur coins.
- **Challenge banner:** koi challenge aaye to upar dikhega, jaise "Rohan ne challenge kiya · Physics" ya "Kota Warriors · Rohan ka gang challenge · 2/5 joined", saath mein Accept/Decline aur bacha hua samay.
- **Stage:** sunehre "RAPID FIRE" title, uske theek neeche ek line "Pen down Fight", phir bada Phoenix Diamond, halka saans leta aur chamakta hua. Uske neeche ribbon mein chuna hua subject aur chapter; tap karne par Physics / Chemistry / Maths aur chapter picker.
- **Stage ke do sticker:** baayein Daily Quests ka chest (reward tayyar ho to badge; Trophy ke Rewards kholta hai), daayein Weak Cards ka nishaana (kitne cards revise karne hain).
- **Mode tiles:** ek line mein teen bade, ubhre hue tiles, har ek par bada 3D icon: **Play Friends** (gang ya dost chunne ki list kholta hai; tile par online doston ki ginti), **Practice Rival**, **Solo Sprint**.
- **Bada teal PLAY button:** bar ke theek upar chipka hua, scroll karne par bhi dikhta hai. Seedha Quick Match shuru karta hai.
- **Gang aur friends ki patti:** pehle aapke gangs (emblem, naam, kitne online), phir doston ke avatar, online wale aage aur hare dot ke saath.
  - gang ko tap karo: chhota card, members aur **Challenge gang** button
  - dost ko tap karo: mini profile, **Challenge** button ke saath
  - "+" se naya dost jodo ya naya gang banao
- **Diamond Vault ki patti:** sabse neeche paanchon diamonds apni ginti ke saath; tap karne par Diamond Vault.

### 5. Phoenix Diamond: streak ki power
- Ek match mein lagatar sahi jawab (Phoenix streak) par Phoenix Diamond jal uthta hai aur diamond milta hai:

| Lagatar sahi | Diamond | Look |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Red | laal ruby |
| 5 | Blue | royal blue, app ki bar wale diamond jaisa |
| 10 | Emerald | gehra hara |
| 15 | Gold | sunehra |
| 20 | Kohinoor | saaf safed, indradhanushi chamak |

- **Kaise milta hai:** padaav paar karte hi us rang ka 1 diamond. Ek lambi streak mein raaste ke saare diamonds milte hain: 20 lagatar sahi par paanchon rang ka ek-ek.
- **Streak tootna:** galat jawab, time khatam ya skip par ginti 0 se. Nayi streak mein diamonds dobara jeete ja sakte hain.
- **Play screen par:**
  - score ke paas streak diamond: pehle bujha hua, ginti ke saath (jaise 2/3).
  - padaav par us rang mein jal uthta hai, aag ke pankh khulte hain, saath mein chamak, vibration aur awaaz.
  - chamak padaav ke saath badhti hai: Red par chhoti, Kohinoor par poori screen.
  - ye sab do cards ke beech hota hai, sawaal ke upar kabhi nahi.
- **Result par:** is match mein jeete diamonds rang ke hisaab se, ek-ek karke Vault mein udte hue, aur is match ki sabse lambi streak.
- **Record (Diamond Vault):**
  - har rang ke kul diamonds aur ab tak ki sabse lambi streak
  - My matches mein har match ke saath us match ke diamonds
  - jo rang abhi nahi mila, wo taale wali parchhai mein
  - Vault Trophy ke Rewards mein aur profile par dikhta hai; doosre players bhi dekh sakte hain
- **Kis khel mein:** Quick Match, dost ya gang ka match, Practice Rival aur Solo Sprint.
- Score, coins aur jeet-haar ke niyam nahi badalte.

### 6. Player identity aur profile
- **Naam:** jo naam app ke account mein hai wahi. Naam na ho to phone number, lekin doosre players ko chhupa hua dikhega (jaise ••••••6276). Poora number sirf aapko dikhega.
- **Profile (apni aur doosron ki):** sunehre frame aur naam ke ribbon wale showcase card mein.
  - **Upar:** bada avatar frame ke saath, naam, level, online status, aur favourite subject se bana title (jaise "Physics Ace").
  - **Buttons:** Add Friend (ek tap, judte hi "Friends ✓"), Challenge, aur dost ho to **Add to gang**.
  - **Stats:** kitne match, jeet, jeet %, sabse lambi jeet ki lakeer, accuracy.
  - **Head-to-head:** kisi aur ki profile par, jaise "Aap 3 – 2 Rohan".
  - **Diamond Vault:** har rang ke kul diamonds aur sabse lambi streak.
  - **Baaki:** trophies, pichle 5 match, aur us player ka entry effect ek tap mein "Watch entry".
  - **Apni profile par:** avatar, frame aur entry badalne ka shortcut, aapki friends list aur aapke gangs.
- **Profile kahan se khulti hai:** kisi bhi avatar ya naam par tap karke:
  - lobby ki patti, Gang tab, leaderboard, history
  - match ke dauran score patti
  - result screen
- **Practice rival:** iski profile par "Practice rival" likha hoga, aur ise add, gang mein ya challenge nahi kar sakte.

### 7. Friends (seedha jodna, approval nahi)
- **Dost jodne ke 4 tareeke:**
  - kisi ki profile par **Add Friend**
  - **Search:** naam se, ya poore phone number se (number exact hona chahiye)
  - match khatam hone par result screen par **Add Friend**
  - apna **invite link** WhatsApp par bhejo. Dost ke tap karte hi app aapki profile par khulti hai.
- **Jab koi aapko jode:** aapko "Rohan ne aapko friend banaya" dikhega, ek tap **Add back** ke saath.
- **Friends list (Gang tab mein):** online wale pehle, phir haal mein active. Har dost ke aage Challenge button.
- **Safety:** profile se **Remove** aur **Block**. Block kiya player aapko jod, dhoondh, gang mein daal ya challenge nahi kar sakta.

### 8. Gang (doston ka shared group)
- **Kya hai:** doston ka ek group, apne naam aur nishaan ke saath. Har member ko gang dikhta hai, aur koi bhi member poore gang ko ek tap mein challenge kar sakta hai.
- **Banana:** Gang tab → **New gang** → naam (20 akshar tak) → 8 emblems mein se nishaan chuno → doston ko jodo.
- **Members jodna:** koi bhi member apne doston ko seedha jod sakta hai, bina approval. Jise joda gaya use dikhega "Rohan ne aapko Kota Warriors mein joda", saath mein **View** aur **Leave**.
- **Kitne:** ek gang mein 20 members tak. Ek player 10 gangs tak mein ho sakta hai.
- **Leader:** gang banane wala leader hota hai. Leader naam aur nishaan badal sakta hai, kisi member ko hata sakta hai aur gang band kar sakta hai. Leader chhod de to sabse purana member leader ban jaata hai.
- **Gang ki screen:**
  - emblem, naam aur kitne online
  - members ki list, online wale pehle
  - bada **Challenge gang** button
  - pichle 5 gang match aur unke winner
  - Add friends, Leave, aur leader ke options
- **Safety:**
  - koi bhi member kabhi bhi **Leave** kar sakta hai.
  - Block kiya player aapko gang mein nahi jod sakta. Wo pehle se aapke gang mein ho, to uske gang challenge aap tak nahi aate.
  - gang ke naam mein gaali wale shabd rok diye jaate hain, aur gang screen par **Report** hota hai.

### 9. Challenge: ek dost, kai dost ya poora gang
- **Kise bhej sakte hain:**
  - ek dost (1 v 1)
  - kai dost ek saath: list se 4 tak chuno
  - poora gang: ek tap
- **Kahan se:** Gang tab, lobby ki patti, profile, ya lobby ka Play Friends tile.
- **Bhejna:** abhi chuna subject/chapter pehle se laga hoga, badal bhi sakte hain → **Send**.
- **Ek match mein kitne:** aapko milakar 5 players tak.
- **Match Room (waiting screen):**
  - 5 seats; har bulaye gaye player ka live status: Bheja → Dekha → Joined, ya Declined.
  - jo jud chuke hain wo room mein ek-doosre ko dekhte hain.
  - countdown (3 minute), Cancel, aur judne wale ke liye Leave.
  - "WhatsApp par yaad dilao" button, jisme seedha is room ko kholne wala link jaata hai (gang ke WhatsApp group mein bhi bheja ja sakta hai).
- **Match kab shuru hota hai:**
  - ek dost bulaya ho: uske Accept karte hi.
  - kai dost ya gang: bhejne wala **START** dabata hai, jab kam se kam 1 dost jud chuka ho.
  - khud shuru: paanchon seats bhar jaayein, ya sab bulaye gaye jawab de chuke hon aur kam se kam 1 juda ho.
  - 3 minute poore hone par: koi juda ho to match unhi ke saath shuru; koi na juda ho to challenge band.
- **Gang mein 5 se zyada log:** jo pehle Accept karte hain unhe seat milti hai. Baaki ko "Match full" dikhta hai.
- **Dost ko kahan dikhega:** challenge banner Home aur saari Rapid Fire screens par aata hai; test dete waqt ya match ke dauran kabhi nahi. Jab tak challenge khula hai, app ki bar ke Rapid Fire diamond par laal dot rehta hai, aur arena ki bar mein Gang icon par ginti.
- **Shuru hote hi:** saare phone ek saath entry show mein jaate hain, phir card 1.
- **Decline ya samay khatam:** bhejne wale ko saaf message, saath mein "Quick Match khelo" aur "Dobara bhejo".
- **Rematch:** result screen par Rematch dabane wala naya Match Room kholta hai, jisme pichhle match ke saare players bulaye jaate hain. Baaki Rematch dabakar ya banner se jud jaate hain. 1 v 1 mein dono ne Rematch dabaya to turant shuru.
- **Ek waqt mein ek challenge:** naya challenge bhejne par aapka pehle wala khula challenge band ho jaata hai.
- **Code se join:** purana 6 akshar wala tareeka Play Friends ke andar backup ki tarah rahega (sirf 1 v 1).

### 10. Powerful entry show (har match mein, har player ke 3.5 second)
- **Kis khel mein:** Quick Match, dost ya gang ka match aur Practice Rival.
- **Do players (lagbhag 9.5 second):**

| Samay | Kya hota hai |
|---|---|
| 0 – 0.5 sec | Screen kaali, halki gadgadahat, beech mein roshni ka ring banta hai |
| 0.5 – 4.0 sec (aap) | Aapka entry effect poori screen par (aag, bijli, galaxy, rocket…). Bada avatar zor se land karta hai, screen hilti hai, tez vibration. Naam patti mein naam, level aur jeet ki ginti. Effect ki apni awaaz. |
| 4.0 – 7.5 sec (rival) | Rival ki entry doosri taraf se, uske effect aur rang mein, aur utni hi zordaar |
| 7.5 – 8.8 sec | Dono aamne-saamne, beech mein Phoenix Diamond ke aage sunehra VS gir kar flash aur jhatke ke saath |
| 8.8 – 9.5 sec | "FIGHT!" aur pehla card |

- **3 se 5 players:** har player ki poori 3.5 second ki entry, ek ke baad ek: apne phone par pehle aap, phir baaki judne ke kram mein. Phir sab ek line mein, beech mein Phoenix Diamond, VS aur "FIGHT!".

| Players | Entry show ka kul samay |
|---|---|
| 2 | lagbhag 9.5 sec |
| 3 | lagbhag 13 sec |
| 4 | lagbhag 16.5 sec |
| 5 | lagbhag 20 sec |

- **Effects:** store ke saare 11 entry effects ke bade full-screen version banenge. Jinke paas koi effect nahi, unhe default "Royal" entry milegi.
- **Sync:** saare phone par show ek saath chalega, aur card 1 sabke liye ek hi pal mein khulega.
- **Animation kam karne wali setting:** in players ko shaant version dikhega (naam pattiyan aur halke fade), lekin samay wahi rahega taaki match barabar rahe.

### 11. Match aur result
- **Match ka format:** 30 cards, har card 15 second (pehle 20). Scoring aur coins wahi. 3 se 5 players ke match mein sab apne liye khelte hain: sabko wahi 30 cards.
- **Play screen:**
  - 1 v 1: upar dono avatar, score aur progress bar.
  - 3 se 5 players: upar score patti mein saare players ke avatar aur score, live rank ke kram mein; aap alag chamakte hue, saath mein aapki jagah (jaise "2nd / 5").
  - sawaal ka card bade, saaf text mein
  - timer ring
  - sahi/galat par turant chamak aur vibration
  - score ke paas streak diamond, jo padaav par jal uthta hai (section 5)
- **Jeet ka niyam (3 se 5 players):** sabse zyada score wala 1st. Score barabar ho to jisne kam samay liya wo aage. Beech mein chhodne wala sabse neeche; baaki khelte rehte hain.
- **Result screen:**
  - 1 v 1: jeet par pankh phailaye Phoenix Diamond, sunehri trophy burst aur "VICTORY", haar par izzat bhara "GOOD FIGHT". Score vs score.
  - 3 se 5 players: podium (1st, 2nd, 3rd) aur poori ranking. 1st ko "VICTORY", baaki ko "GOOD FIGHT" unki jagah ke saath (jaise "2nd of 5").
  - is match mein jeete diamonds, rang ke hisaab se ginti, aur sabse lambi streak.
  - coins/XP ginti ke saath badhte hue.
  - bade buttons: Rematch, Add Friend (jo dost nahi hain unke aage), Profile dekho, Galat cards dekho, Done.

## User flow
1. **Rapid Fire kholna:** app ki bar ke diamond par tap → crimson arena ki lobby khulti hai, neeche arena ki apni bar.
2. **Dost ko challenge (1 v 1):**
   - lobby ki patti ya Gang tab mein dost ko tap karo → Challenge → Send.
   - Match Room mein "Dekha" ke baad "Joined" aata hai.
   - entry show (9.5 sec) → match → result → Rematch.
3. **Gang banana:** Gang tab → New gang → naam aur nishaan → doston ko jodo. Unhe "aapko joda gaya" dikhta hai.
4. **Gang ya kai doston ko challenge:**
   - Gang tab mein gang par **Challenge gang**, ya friends list se 4 tak dost chuno → Send.
   - Match Room mein seats bharti dikhti hain (jaise 3/5 joined).
   - START dabao, ya sab jud jaayein to khud shuru.
   - entry show (har player 3.5 sec) → sabke liye wahi 30 cards → podium wala result.
5. **Challenge milna:** Home ya Rapid Fire par banner → Accept → Match Room → entry show → match.
6. **Naya dost banana:**
   - match ke baad result par Add Friend, ya
   - Gang tab → Search → Add, ya
   - WhatsApp invite link → dost app kholta hai → aapki profile → Add.
7. **Quick Match:**
   - Play → chamakta radar talaash (lagbhag 6 sec tak)
   - asli player ya label laga Practice Rival milta hai
   - entry show → match
8. **Diamonds jeetna:** match mein 3 lagatar sahi → Red diamond jal uthta hai → streak chalti rahe to 5 par Blue, 10 par Emerald, 15 par Gold, 20 par Kohinoor → result par ginti → Diamond Vault mein judte hain.
9. **Trophy aur Store:** arena bar se ek tap. Trophy mein leaderboard, quest ka inaam Claim, Diamond Vault, trophies aur My matches; Store mein avatars, entry effects aur frames.
10. **Arena se bahar:** bar mein Home → app ka Home.
11. **Profile dekhna:** kisi bhi avatar par tap → stats, head-to-head, trophies, Watch entry, Add Friend / Challenge / Add to gang.

## UI/UX feel
- **Mahaul:** beautiful aur powerful, jaise kisi bade mobile game ka arena: rangeen, ubhra hua, zinda. Lekin bheed nahi: har screen par ek hi bada kaam.
- **Rang:**
  - crimson laal: har arena screen ka background
  - teal: sirf dabane wale buttons
  - sona: sirf jeet, rewards aur khaas frame
  - diamonds ke paanch rang: sirf diamonds par
- **Gehrai:** 3D icons, ubhre hue panel aur parchhai, taaki har cheez chhoone layak lage. Patli line wale icons kahin nahi.
- **Text aur haath:** bade bold numbers aur saaf text. Main buttons neeche, angoothe ki pahunch mein.
- **Motion:** buttons dabte hain, tiles halka uchhalte hain, Phoenix Diamond saans leta hai, PLAY par roshni ki lakeer chalti hai. Match Room mein seat bharne par halka pop. Tez flash sirf entry show, naye diamond aur jeet par.
- **Mehsoos hona:** zaroori palon par vibration aur sound (sound setting ke hisaab se).
- **Padhne mein aasaan:** sawaal padhna hamesha pehli priority. Card par contrast tez, koi animation sawaal ke upar nahi. 5 players ki score patti bhi sawaal ki jagah nahi leti.

   - naam aur nishaan ke saath shared gang banana
   - koi bhi member apne doston ko jode; "aapko joda gaya" notice
   - gang ki screen: members, online, pichle 5 match
   - leader ke options, Leave, Report
6. Challenge:
   - ek dost, kai dost (4 tak) ya poora gang

## Assumptions
- **Reference tasveerein:** pankh wala laal heera, 8 Ball Pool aur chess.com ke screenshots sirf prerna hain. Inme se koi tasveer ya icon seedha nahi lagega.
- **Artwork:** Phoenix Diamond, paanch diamonds, 3D icons, gang emblems aur background ke nishaan apne original banenge, AI image model (Gemini Nano Banana) se, jaise pehle avatars aur bar ka diamond bane. Isme universal key ke thode credits ek baar lagenge. App chalte waqt koi image nahi banti.
- **App ka size:** saara naya artwork milakar lagbhag 0.6 MB ke andar, taaki app 20 MB se neeche rahe.
- **Rang ke niyam:** crimson background, teal buttons, sunehre headings, aqua chhoti line, peele badge. Purple kahin nahi.
- **Arena bar:** baayein se daayein Home, Trophy, Arena (Phoenix Diamond), Gang, Store. Diye gaye kram (2nd Trophy, 3rd Gang, 4th Store) mein Phoenix Diamond ko beech mein rakha gaya hai, kyunki wahi lobby kholta hai.
- **Trophy ke andar:** Ranks, Rewards (quests, daily streak, Diamond Vault, trophies) aur My matches teeno. Alag Rewards icon bar mein nahi hai.
- **Gang icon kahan:** sirf arena ki bar mein. App ki apni bar (Home, Rank, Rapid Fire, My Tests, Mission) nahi badlegi; wahan se Rapid Fire ke diamond se arena, phir Gang ek tap.
- **Store:** bar mein bhi hai, aur har arena screen par coins ke + se bhi khulta hai.
- **App ki bar:** Rapid Fire ke andar nahi dikhti. Rank, My Tests aur Mission ke liye pehle Home par jaana hoga (ek tap).
- **Bar kab chhupti hai:** talaash, Match Room, entry show, match aur result ke dauran.
- **Max 5:** ek match mein aapko milakar 5 players (aap + 4).
- **3 se 5 players ka match:** sab apne liye. Do teams wala match Phase 2 mein.
- **Group match mein kaun:** sirf asli dost ya gang ke members. Practice Rival group match mein nahi aata. Quick Match 1 v 1 hi rahega.
- **Group mein jeet:** sirf 1st aane wale ko jeet ka bonus aur "jeet" ki ginti milti hai (kam se kam 5 sahi jawab ka maujooda niyam laagu). Har sahi jawab ke coins sabko pehle jaise.
- **Stats:** group match ek match gina jaata hai. Head-to-head sirf 1 v 1 matches se banta hai.
- **Gang ka size:** 20 members tak; ek player 10 gangs tak. Gang 5 se bada ho to match mein pehle Accept karne walon ko seat milti hai.
- **Gang mein jodna:** koi bhi member apne doston ko bina approval jod sakta hai. Hatana, naam/nishaan badalna aur gang band karna sirf leader kar sakta hai.
- **Gang challenge kaun bhej sakta hai:** gang ka koi bhi member, chahe saare members aapas mein dost na hon.
- **Gang ka naam:** 20 akshar tak. Gaali wale shabd rok diye jaate hain; Report ki gayi gangs owner ke dekhne ke liye darj hoti hain.
- **Challenge ka link:** sirf bulaye gaye players (ya us gang ke members) ke liye khulta hai.
- **Entry show ka samay:** har player ke poore 3.5 second, har match mein, bina skip. 2 players par lagbhag 9.5 sec, 5 players par lagbhag 20 sec. Animation kam wali setting mein bhi samay wahi.
- **Entry ka kram:** apne phone par pehle apni entry, phir baaki players judne ke kram mein. Kul samay sabke phone par barabar.
- **Entry show kab:** Quick Match, dost ya gang ka match aur Practice Rival. Solo Sprint seedha shuru hoga.
- **Default entry:** jinke paas koi khareeda hua entry effect nahi, unhe "Royal" entry milegi.
- **Do alag streak:** Daily streak (roz khelne ki, pehle jaisi) aur Phoenix streak (ek match ke andar lagatar sahi jawab). Diamonds sirf Phoenix streak se milte hain.
- **Phoenix streak ki ginti:** har match mein 0 se shuru. Galat jawab, time khatam ya skip par toot-ti hai.
- **Diamonds kitne:** har padaav paar karne par us rang ka 1 diamond. Streak tootne ke baad wahi rang dobara jeeta ja sakta hai, isliye ek match mein ek rang kai baar mil sakta hai.
- **Diamonds ka mol:** abhi sirf collection aur record. Inse score, coins ya jeet-haar nahi badalti. Diamonds se kharidari Phase 2 mein.
- **Beech mein chhodna:** diamonds match poora hone par hi judte hain. Khud match chhodne par us match ke diamonds nahi milte.
- **Weak cards revision:** isme diamonds nahi milte, kyunki wahi cards dobara aate hain.
- **Purane match:** diamonds ki ginti is update ke baad ke khelon se shuru hogi. Purane match dobara nahi gine jaayenge.
- **Vault kaun dekhe:** har player ka Diamond Vault uski profile par sabko dikhta hai.
- **Baaki players ki streak:** match ke dauran sirf apna streak diamond dikhta hai. Doosron ka Phase 2 mein.
- **Celebration ka samay:** diamond ki chamak do cards ke beech ke maujooda gap mein hi hoti hai. Match ka samay nahi badhta.
- **Animation kam wali setting:** saans, uchhaal aur flash band. Rang, layout aur samay wahi.
- **App ke andar bhasha:** buttons aur labels English mein, jaise abhi hain.
- **15 second kahan:** har Rapid Fire khel mein: Quick Match, dost ya gang ka match, Practice Rival, Solo Sprint aur weak cards revision. Aakhri 5 second ki tick-tick pehle jaisi.
- **20 second ka zikr:** app ke andar Rapid Fire mein jahan bhi 20 second likha hai, wahan 15 hoga. Pehle bane launch videos nahi badlenge.
- **"Pen down Fight" line:** shabd wahi jo diye gaye. Sirf lobby mein, RAPID FIRE title ke theek neeche, halke aqua rang mein.
- **Naam:** account ka naam dikhega. Naam na ho to phone number, lekin doosre players ko chhupa hua (sirf aakhri 4 ank), taaki koi anjaan call na kar sake. Poora number sirf aapko dikhega.
- **Friend jodna:** ek taraf se aur turant hota hai. Jise aap jodte hain wo aapki list mein aata hai, aur use ek-tap "Add back" ka notice milta hai.
- **Kaun challenge kar sakta hai:** jo bhi player aapko jod chuka hai, aur aapke gang ka koi bhi member. Aap Decline, Remove, Leave ya Block kar sakte hain.
- **Challenge kaise pahunchta hai:** sirf app ke andar. Banner Home aur Rapid Fire par, aur laal dot / ginti. Jo dost us waqt app mein nahi hai, use WhatsApp link se bulaya ja sakta hai.
- **Expire:** challenge 3 minute mein band ho jaata hai, agar tab tak koi juda na ho.
- **Match:** 30 cards hi rahenge, har card 15 second (pehle 20). Scoring aur coins wahi. Dost ya gang ka match bhejne wale ke chune subject/chapter par hoga.
- **Practice rival:** saaf label ke saath dikhte hain, aur inhe dost ya gang member nahi banaya ja sakta.
- **Data:** profiles, friends, gangs, challenges aur diamonds Rapid Fire ke apne system mein rahenge, website par nahi, jaise aaj Rapid Fire ka baaki data.
- **Purane features:** store, quests, streak, trophies, leaderboard, weak revision, history, Solo Sprint aur code se join sab rahenge. Sirf unka look aur jagah badlegi.
- **Baaki app:** Home aur tests light hi rahenge. Crimson arena sirf Rapid Fire mein. App ki bar ka royal-blue diamond button jaisa hai waisa hi rahega.
- **Purane theme examples:** Gold, Midnight aur Purple wale teen examples aur unka chooser screen hata diye jaayenge.
- **Invite link:** jinke phone mein app nahi hai, unhe ek page dikhega jisme code aur download button hoga.
